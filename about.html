About the Book

LILITH BLACKWELL is a dark magic-tech war epic, divine psychological horror, and villain-protagonist saga.

The World

In 2039, scientists discovered Aether — a new fundamental force that reacts directly to human thought, belief, and intent. Religion claimed it was divine. Science proved it was undiscovered physics. Both were right.

When billions of minds believed in gods over centuries, Aether condensed around those beliefs. It formed spirits, angels, miracles... and finally, God. But God is not the creator. He is the largest Aether thought-form ever made — a self-sustaining parasitic intelligence born from fear, worship, hope, and desperation.

The War

The United States weaponized Aether. Ley-lines became power grids. Souls were bound into weapons. Angels were reverse-engineered into batteries. Prayer was converted into artillery. World War III doesn't burn cities — it makes them stop existing correctly.

The Protagonist

Lilith Rose Blackwell — former defense lawyer, current Director of the US Aether-war machine. She defended war criminals and won. She died, met God, and gave Him nothing. Now she exploits every loophole in divine law, military doctrine, and the Geneva Convention.

She asks: "What is the worst thing I can do that is still technically legal?"

Then she does it.

Content Warnings

⚠️ Villain protagonist with no redemption arc. War crimes (procedurally compliant). Psychological horror. Religious deconstruction. Extreme violence. Moral nihilism played straight.
