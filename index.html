Enter the War

Dark Magic-Tech War Epic

In 2020, humanity discovered Aether — a force shaped by consciousness. The "pandemic" was a lie. The lockdowns were containment. The deaths were real, but not from any virus. They weaponized it. They built empires with it. And they accidentally proved that God is not the creator... but the largest thought-form ever made. A parasite feeding on belief.

One woman refuses to kneel. A defense lawyer with no empathy and perfect legal precision. She joins the honest monsters, exploits every loophole in divine law, and wages procedural warfare against Heaven itself.

⚠️ Villain protagonist. War crimes. Psychological horror. No redemption arcs.
