# Characters

## Lilith Rose Blackwell

**Titles:**
- To the US: *Director Blackwell*
- To enemies: *The Dominion Witch*
- To believers: *The Heretic Engine*
- To God: *"The One Who Refuses"*

**Note:** She despises her middle name. Anyone lower in rank who calls her "Rose" instead of her full name earns a death glare that ends careers.

### Original Life

Before God killed her, Lilith was the best defense lawyer on Earth.

**She defended:**
- War criminals
- Corporate genociders
- Human traffickers
- Bio-weapon executives
- Private military firms

**Her method:**
She never asked: *"Did you do it?"*
She asked: *"Can they prove it under their own rules?"*

And she won.

**Core belief formed here:**
> "If I follow the rules better than anyone else, I deserve to exist."

### Death & Resurrection

Lilith died. God appeared, expecting fear, prayer, or worship.

She gave Him nothing.

God sent her to a world where He was undeniably real — believing it would force her to kneel.

**It failed.**

Lilith saw:
- Physics
- Aether
- Parasites
- Systems

Not divinity.

God became obsessed with breaking her.

### Why She Sides with Evil

The empire says: *"We will use you. You will kill. We will give you power."*

God says: *"I love you. You owe me."*

**She chooses the honest monster.**

### Psychology

Lilith is insane — not emotionally, but **morally**.

- She feels no guilt
- She feels **optimization**

To her:
- Pain is a resource
- Death is a statistic  
- Ethics are loopholes

She applies her lawyer brain to:
- The Geneva Convention
- Military doctrine
- Divine law
- Angelic commandments

She asks: *"What is the worst thing I can do that is still technically legal?"*

Then she does it.

**Examples:**
- Starving cities ✓ Legal
- Using civilians as shields ✓ Legal
- Forcing saints to break their holy laws ✓ Legal

Every atrocity is:
- Documented
- Justified
- Procedurally compliant

### Her Core Goal

> "The being calling itself God is a fake. I will end this war with my own hands so the universe can see it."

She is running a universe-scale experiment. If God cannot stop her... He is not God.
