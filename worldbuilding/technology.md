# Aether Technology

## Core Principles

Aether reacts to human thought, belief, and intent.

Technology in this world merges:
- Traditional engineering
- Consciousness interfaces
- Weaponized faith

---

## Weapons

### Soul-Bound Weapons
- **Description**: Human souls imprisoned as power sources
- **How they work**: The soul's emotional energy is converted to Aether output
- **Ethical status**: Condemned by religions; legally ambiguous under secular law
- **Examples**: The most powerful AETHCOM weapons require willing (or coerced) volunteers

### Prayer Artillery
- **Description**: Collective belief converted to destructive force
- **How it's aimed**: Aether resonance chambers focus prayer energy from thousands
- **Source**: Captured believers, fanatics, or unwitting congregations
- **Effect**: Varies based on what the prayers ask for — destruction prayers hit hardest

### Angel Batteries
- **Description**: Divine beings reverse-engineered into energy cells
- **Extraction process**: Painful, takes weeks, requires specialized containment
- **Power output**: One angel = enough energy for a small city for months
- **Side effect**: The angel remains conscious and in agony throughout

### Sanctification Bombs
- **Description**: Reality-rewriting weapons of last resort
- **Effect**: Targets don't burn — they stop existing correctly
- **Horror factor**: Survivors describe buildings that go "somewhere else," people who become "not quite there"
- **Status**: Never used in populated areas (officially)

---

## Military Hardware

### Magic Aircraft Carriers
- **Description**: AETHCOM mobile command bases
- **Features**: Dimensionally shielded, can partially phase out of normal space
- **Stealth**: Invisible to conventional radar and satellite
- **Crew**: Mix of conventional sailors and Aether-capable operators

### Dimensional Strike Portals
- **Description**: Instant force projection anywhere on Earth
- **Limitations**: Require massive power (usually an angel battery)
- **Duration**: Can remain open for 60-90 seconds maximum
- **Risk**: Portal collapse in active use is catastrophic

### Thought-Controlled Spell Processors
- **Description**: Mental interface combat systems implanted in operators
- **Function**: Convert conscious intent into directed Aether effects
- **Training**: 6 months minimum to achieve basic competency
- **Side effects**: Some operators report "bleed" of emotions from previous users

### The Harbinger (Lilith's Sidearm)
- **Type**: Aether-core military pistol
- **Classification**: Black site equipment only
- **Features**: Chambers infused with Aether; bullets carry user's intent
- **Note**: Used in the Prologue; emptied during the angel fight

## Infrastructure

### Ley-Line Power Grids
- Natural Aether channels exist across Earth
- AETHCOM mapped them and built infrastructure to tap them
- Major cities unknowingly run on Aether-supplemented power
- Greenland's arctic ley-lines made it strategically critical

## Personal Tech

### Natural Aether Resistance

Some individuals develop natural resistance to divine influence through sheer willpower and Aether mastery. This is extremely rare.

**Lilith Blackwell** is the prime example:
- Her will over Aether grew faster than God predicted
- She is effectively invisible to divine omniscience
- No implants or technology — pure willpower
- This makes her unique and terrifying

### Standard AETHCOM Gear

| Equipment | Function |
|-----------|----------|
| Tactical jackets | Light Aether shielding, ballistic protection |
| Reality anchors | Prevent localized divine reality manipulation |
| Aether goggles | Visualize Aether flows and divine presences |
| Comm implants | Secure communication, basic Aether detection |

### Lilith's Personal Equipment
- **Combat knife**: Seven inches, blackened blade, channels her violet Aether
- **The Harbinger**: Aether-core sidearm (see above)
- **Tactical jacket**: Standard issue, torn in the Prologue
- **No implants**: She relies entirely on natural ability

---

## Civilian Applications

Aether technology is almost entirely military/classified. Civilian applications exist but are disguised:
- "Smart" devices with minor Aether sensitivity (marketed as AI)
- Medical scanners that detect Aether potential (the "health screenings")
- Power grid efficiency (attributed to "green energy improvements")

The public has no idea how much of modern infrastructure runs on Aether.
