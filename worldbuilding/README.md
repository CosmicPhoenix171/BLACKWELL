## ⚡ Quick Reference

### Core Premise
- **Timeline**: 2020 onward (Aether discovered during "pandemic")
- **The Discovery**: Aether — a fundamental force shaped by consciousness
- **The Truth**: God is a parasitic thought-form created by collective belief
- **His Limitation**: God can only do what people *believe* He can do
- **The Conflict**: US Aether-tech war machine vs. divine entities
- **The Protagonist**: Lilith Blackwell — villain, tactician, "The One Who Refuses"

## 🔑 Core Rules

### Aether Mechanics
1. Aether reacts to consciousness — thought, belief, intent
2. Collective belief over time creates thought-forms (including God)
3. Individual willpower can shield against divine influence
4. Lilith's absorbed Aether makes her invisible to God's omniscience

### God's Cage
1. God can only do what people believe He can do
2. He cannot force belief — must work through miracles and appearances
3. He is currently expanding his power through public appearances
4. Every divine death erodes collective faith

### Lilith's Immunity
1. She naturally absorbs Aether, stockpiling it within her body
2. Through training, she learned to use willpower to shape and release stored Aether
3. She cannot be erased, read, or affected directly by God
4. God must send agents (angels, saints, heroes) instead
5. She studies divine rules and exploits their weaknesses

## ✍️ Writing Guidelines

### Tone
- Dark, procedural, clinical when describing violence
- No redemption arcs — Lilith is a villain protagonist
- Tactical/strategic language when discussing warfare and divine rules
- Horror elements come from the implications, not gore

### Consistency Checks
- Cross-reference with [plot-holes.md](plot-holes.md) when adding new lore
- All Aether mechanics must follow the belief→power→limitation framework
- Lilith's knowledge should reflect her strategic training and Aether study
