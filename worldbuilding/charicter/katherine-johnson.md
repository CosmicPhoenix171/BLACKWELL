# Katherine "Kat" Johnson

**Callsign:** Owl (assigned, not earned — she hates it)

**Titles:**
- To AETHCOM: *Probationary Conscript Johnson*
- To the team: *The Loud One* / *Kid*
- To herself: *Someone who shouldn't be here*

**Team Role:** Designated Marksman / Overwatch

**Note:** Call her "sweetie" or "kid" and she'll make you regret it. Call her out on being scared and she'll double down until she gets herself killed.

---

## Background

Katherine Johnson was 22 years old when the draft notice arrived. No warning. No prep. No buy-in.

One day she was a civilian. The next day she was government property.

**Before the Draft:**
- Normal American upbringing, nothing military
- No family service history
- No interest in combat, patriotism, or sacrifice
- Was figuring out her life when the world ended

**What Made Her Different:**
Aether.

Kat tested positive during the mandatory screening sweeps. High latent potential — the kind of numbers that make AETHCOM recruiters show up at your door with paperwork and no patience for questions.

She didn't ask for it. Didn't want it. Doesn't understand it. But the government doesn't care what civilians want when they're sitting on weapons-grade potential.

She's what happens when you draft someone for what they *are* instead of who they choose to be.

## Aether Profile

**Classification:** Latent / Developing

**Potential Rating:** High (exact metrics classified)

**Manifestation Type:** Perception — enhanced sight, threat awareness, precognitive flashes

**Current Control:** Minimal — instinctive flares under stress

**Training Status:** Early stages; struggling with focus and emotional regulation

**How It Manifests:**
- Sees things before they happen — fractions of a second, sometimes longer
- Threat awareness that borders on paranoia (hard to tell where instinct ends and Aether begins)
- When focused: can track targets through walls, predict movement patterns
- When panicked: sensory overload, sees too much, freezes

**Why Sniper:**
AETHCOM identified her manifestation type during screening. Perception-class Anomalies make ideal marksmen — if they can learn to control the input. The problem is Kat can't turn it off. She sees threats everywhere because her Aether *shows* her threats everywhere.

**Key Detail:**
Kat's Aether responds to fear and anger. Which means it responds constantly. She's a live wire nobody's figured out how to ground yet. The scope is supposed to teach her focus. So far, it's teaching her how many ways she can die.

**What She Knows:**
- She tested positive
- They want her because of what she sees
- It scares her more than the angels do

**What She Doesn't Know:**
- How far her perception could extend with training
- That precognitive Anomalies often burn out or go mad
- Why AETHCOM really assigned her to Lilith's team

## Psychological Profile

**Core Traits:**
- Unfiltered — says what she thinks, usually at the worst time
- Impulsive — reacts before thinking
- Defensive — interprets everything as an attack
- Sharp-tongued — covers fear with sarcasm
- Civilian-rude — hasn't learned military respect (and resents being expected to)

**Emotional State:**
- Unbroken
- Unhardened
- Still believes she might go home when this is over

**Stress Response:**
- Snaps verbally first
- Gets reckless when cornered
- Refuses help even when she desperately needs it
- Makes bad jokes at the worst possible moments
- Freezes for half a second before moving — the hesitation that gets people killed

**Core Fear:**
Being powerless — and being powerful. Both terrify her.

**Core Lie She Believes:**
> *"If I act tough enough, nothing can touch me."*

**Secondary Fear:**
That the Aether inside her is more real than she is. That they only see the weapon, not the person.

**What She Hasn't Learned:**
Anger is armor. Sarcasm is a shield. Neither works as well as she thinks.

## Relationship with Lilith

Kat doesn't know what to make of Lilith Blackwell.

**What She Sees:**
- Someone who doesn't flinch
- Someone who knows exactly what she's doing
- Someone terrifying

**What She Feels:**
- Intimidated
- Resentful
- Curious despite herself

**What She Doesn't Understand:**
Lilith looks at her and sees someone worth protecting. Not because Kat is special — because Kat is *everyone*. Every civilian who got dragged into this. Every person who wasn't ready.

Lilith is fighting a war so people like Kat don't have to.

Kat has no idea.

## Narrative Role

- **Audience surrogate** in early chapters — sees the world fresh, asks the questions readers have
- **Shows the cost of drafting Aether-gifted civilians** — what happens when you weaponize people who weren't ready
- **Friction point for veterans** like Doc Mercer — her attitude grates, but they remember being young
- **Potential growth arc** from raw to lethal — if she survives long enough
- **Represents who Lilith is trying to protect** — and failing to reach
- **Mirror to Lilith** — both Aether-gifted, but where Lilith embraced her power, Kat fears hers
- **The team's eyes** — when she learns control, she'll see threats before anyone else. Until then, she's a liability in overwatch

She's not comic relief. She's not a redemption project. She's a person thrown into hell and coping the only way she knows how.

---

## Character Arc Potential

**Phase 1: Raw**
- Loud, defensive, making mistakes
- Can't control what she sees — sensory overload
- Misses shots because she flinches before the trigger pull
- Survives on luck and others covering for her

**Phase 2: Breaking**
- First real loss — someone she saw die before it happened and couldn't stop
- Realizes seeing the future doesn't mean you can change it
- Goes quiet. Starts actually listening.

**Phase 3: Hardening**
- Learns to narrow the input. Focus. Breathe.
- First clean kill at distance — doesn't celebrate
- Becomes what she hated: someone who sees death coming and pulls the trigger anyway

**Phase 4: ?**
- Does she keep her humanity?
- Does she burn out like other precogs?
- Does she become the team's most valuable asset — or their biggest threat?
