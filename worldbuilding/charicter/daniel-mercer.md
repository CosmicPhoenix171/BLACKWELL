# Daniel "Doc" Mercer

**Callsign:** Topper

**Titles:**
- To AETHCOM: *Contractor Mercer*
- To operators who served with him: *Doc*
- To Lilith: *The one who doesn't flinch*

**Note:** Call him a hero and he'll walk out of the room. Call him useful and he might stick around.

---

## Background

Daniel Mercer was a combat medic for ten years. SEAL Teams. Black ops. The kind of missions that don't exist on paper and leave scars that don't show on the body.

He could have climbed the ranks. They recommended him for advancement more than once. He declined every time. Paperwork and command politics meant less time with his hands in someone's chest cavity, keeping them alive long enough to see their family again.

That was all he ever wanted to do. Save lives.

Then Aether took the one life he couldn't save.

**Before Aether:**
- Midwestern upbringing, working-class family
- Enlisted young, found purpose in medicine under fire
- Married his high school sweetheart after his third deployment
- Planned to have kids, build a life, get out clean

**What Changed:**
His wife died from Aether exposure. Not in combat. Not in some heroic last stand. Collateral contamination. Wrong place, wrong time, wrong universe.

He held her hand while the doctors explained there was nothing they could do.

Ten years of pulling teammates back from the edge, and he couldn't do a damn thing for her.

## Psychological Profile

**Core Traits:**
- Calm under pressure — the calmest person in any room that's falling apart
- Dry, understated humor — never laughs at his own jokes
- Emotionally closed-off — opens up to no one
- Loyal to the point of self-destruction

**Trauma Factors:**
- Wife died from Aether exposure / contamination
- No children, despite wanting them badly
- Carries guilt for surviving both the war and her
- Believes he should have been able to save her

**Coping Mechanisms:**
- Alcohol (whiskey, usually cheap)
- Isolation (pushes people away before they can leave)
- Work that proves he's still useful (takes dangerous jobs)

**Mental State:**
Not suicidal — just tired.

He isn't trying to die. He just doesn't care much if he doesn't make it. There's a difference. Most people don't understand it.

Doc does.

## Relationship with Lilith

Doc is assigned to AETHCOM's high-value asset medical support. That's the official designation.

Unofficially, he's the one who stitches Lilith back together after she does things that should kill her.

**What He Sees:**
- A weapon wearing a woman's face
- Someone who's going to get herself killed
- Someone who might be worth saving anyway

**What He Doesn't Say:**
- She reminds him of operators he couldn't bring home
- He's seen what happens when people stop caring if they live
- He recognizes the emptiness because he carries his own

**Dynamic:**
- He doesn't lecture her
- He doesn't judge her
- He patches her up, hands her coffee, and waits
- Sometimes that's enough

## Narrative Role

- **Grounded counterweight** to overpowered characters
- **Moral compass** who's seen the cost of war up close
- **Understands what Lilith is becoming** — and what it takes to come back

He's not here to save her soul. He's here to keep her body functional long enough for her to figure out if she has one.
